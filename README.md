# Zombie Shooter 3D

A browser-based 3D FPS zombie survival game built with Three.js.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or play on [GitHub Pages](https://khannudomvirak.github.io/Zombie-Shooter/).

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| WASD | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| Left Click | Shoot |
| Shift | Sprint |
| 1-4 / Scroll | Switch weapon |
| R | Reload |
| Escape | Pause |

## Weapons

| Weapon | Damage | Fire Rate | Magazine | Reserve |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Pistol | 1 | Semi | 12 | 36 |
| SMG | 0.6 | Auto | 25 | 100 |
| Shotgun | 2×3 pellets | Semi | 4 | 16 |
| Sniper | 5 | Semi | 3 | 12 |

Zombies drop ammo and health pickups on death.

## Difficulty

Three modes — Easy, Normal, Hard — affecting zombie damage, count, and spawn rate.
